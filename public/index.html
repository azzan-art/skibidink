Court etiquette

SkibiDink house rules

A few rules to keep the court in good shape for everyone.

Court-ready footwear

Wear clean, non-marking court shoes to protect the playing surface and give yourself secure movement. Please leave outdoor grit and damaging soles off the court.

Equipment etiquette

Please handle nets, posts and rental paddles with care. Report any damage to staff and help keep our gear game-ready.

Respect booking hours

Please arrive 10 minutes before your slot. Start and end on time so everyone gets their fair share of court time.

Food off the court

Outside food is welcome, but outside drinks are not allowed. Please keep all food and drinks off the playing court to keep it clean and safe for everyone.

FAQs

Questions before you play?

Here’s what to know before you make SkibiDink your next stop.

Do you have paddles and balls to rent?

Yes. Rental paddles and balls are offered on a first-come, first-served basis. Just ask our staff on-site.

What’s your cancellation and refund policy?

Once booked, it’s locked in. All reservations are non-refundable, so make sure your schedule is ready to dink.

Can I drop in by myself?

Solo players are welcome during suitable open-play periods. Check the booking board or contact SkibiDink for current availability and the best time to join a game.

How do I reserve a court?

Use the booking page to send your reservation.

What are SkibiDink’s operating hours?

We’re open daily from 6 AM to 11 PM. Early-bird dinks or late-night rallies, we’ve got you.

Is parking available?

Yes. You can park outside the main gate or bring your ride inside, subject to available space.
