    Court etiquette

    SkibiDink house rules

    Help us keep the courts clean, safe and friendly for everyone who plays here.

    Proper footwear

    Non-marking court or athletic shoes only, to protect the playing surface.

    Respect booking hours

    Arrive a few minutes early and clear the court on time so the next players can start.

    No food on courts

    Keep snacks and sugary drinks off court. Water and sports drinks are fine.

    Equipment care

    Treat nets, posts and rental paddles with care, and report any damage to staff.

    Have questions?

    Frequently asked questions

    Everything you need to know about bookings, equipment and the courts.

    Do I need to bring my own paddles and balls?

    No. Paddles and balls are available to rent at the front desk. You are welcome to bring your own too.

    How do I reserve a court?

    Online booking is coming to this site. Until then, message us to hold a slot.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Our cancellation policy will be posted here once bookings open.

    I have never played. Can I still come?

    Absolutely. Pickleball is quick to pick up, and our regulars are happy to show new players the basics.
