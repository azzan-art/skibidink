Court etiquette

SkibiDink house rules

A few rules to keep the court in good shape for everyone.

Proper footwear

Non-marking court or athletic shoes only, to protect the playing surface.

Respect booking hours

Arrive a few minutes early and clear the court on time so the next players can start.

No food on the court

Keep snacks and sugary drinks off the court. Water and sports drinks are fine.

Equipment care

Treat the net, posts and paddles with care, and report any damage.
